Adventure leader sign-up. Each meeting needs two parents to lead the adventure with the den leader. Pick a month that works for you.

How it works: tap Sign up under Adventure Leader #1 or #2, enter your first name, and save. You can remove your own sign-up if plans change.

Want to lead a different adventure? Each month shows a suggested adventure, but you're welcome to propose another. Tap Suggest a different adventure above the leader spots, enter the adventure and your first name, and save. The den leader will follow up with you.

Rows marked tentative are penciled in and may shift. The den leader will send materials and ideas about two weeks before your meeting.
